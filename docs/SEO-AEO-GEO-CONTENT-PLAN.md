# SEO, AEO and GEO Content Plan

## Primary positioning

Dr Tammi Quek Developmental and Behavioural Paediatrics Clinic is a Singapore clinic at Novena Medical Center. The website should explain the clinic's scope clearly, answer family questions in natural language and demonstrate an individualised, whole-child approach.

## Core Singapore search themes

- developmental paediatrician Singapore
- developmental and behavioural paediatrics Singapore
- child development assessment Singapore
- ADHD assessment child Singapore
- autism assessment child Singapore
- speech delay developmental paediatrician Singapore
- developmental delay consultation Singapore
- learning difficulties child Singapore
- child behaviour consultation Singapore
- school concerns developmental paediatrician Singapore
- Novena developmental paediatrician
- Dr Tammi Quek

Use these themes naturally in page titles, headings, answers, image alternative text and internal links. Do not repeat keywords unnaturally or create pages only to target a phrase.

## Regional and international-family themes

The strongest regional page is `/international-families/`. It should naturally include:

- developmental paediatrician Singapore for international families
- child developmental assessment Singapore from Indonesia
- developmental paediatrician Singapore for Filipino families
- developmental paediatrician Singapore for Malaysian families
- Singapore developmental clinic for Southeast Asian families
- preparing medical and school reports before travelling to Singapore

Do not create thin, nearly identical country doorway pages. Add a separate country page only when there is enough genuinely different information about travel, documentation, language, referral pathways or family needs.

## AEO content principles

- Begin each page with a direct explanation of what the page answers.
- Use descriptive headings written as real family questions or needs.
- Keep important answers in visible HTML rather than only inside images or scripts.
- Define terms such as developmental paediatric consultation in plain language.
- State limitations clearly: no online diagnosis, no automatic booking and no emergency service.
- Include location, telephone, address and clinic specialty in crawlable text.
- Link related questions to the most relevant detailed page.

## GEO and generative-search principles

- Prioritise original clinic-specific information, including the manual screening process, school coordination and Dr Tammi's stated whole-child philosophy.
- Use first-hand clinic photographs with descriptive alternative text.
- Keep Dr Tammi's credentials, clinic address and contact information consistent across the website and Google Business Profile.
- Maintain clear authorship and clinical responsibility on health-information pages.
- Add review dates when future articles are clinically reviewed.
- Avoid generic mass-produced condition pages and unsupported claims.
- Do not claim guaranteed outcomes, superiority, instant assessment or AI diagnosis.

## International enquiry conversion

The international page should answer these questions clearly:

1. Can a family outside Singapore enquire?
2. What information should be sent first?
3. Should flights be booked before confirmation?
4. May more than one visit be required?
5. Can school reports and professional assessments be reviewed?
6. How will appointment dates be arranged?

The website should repeat that submitting an enquiry does not confirm an appointment and that the clinic team coordinates dates manually.

## KIMI marketing tool

Position KIMI as an educational guide and enquiry-preparation assistant. Appropriate functions include:

- directing visitors to relevant clinic pages
- helping families organise broad questions
- explaining what records may be useful
- drafting a short non-identifying enquiry summary

It must not be presented as:

- an online developmental assessment
- a diagnostic chatbot
- a treatment recommendation service
- an emergency or crisis service
- an appointment-booking system

Display a privacy notice advising users not to enter a child's full name, identification number, exact date of birth, medical record number, passwords or detailed sensitive records into a third-party AI service.
