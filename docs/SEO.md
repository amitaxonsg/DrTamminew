# SEO, AEO and generative-search strategy

## Core principles

The website uses visible, useful, people-first pages. It does not use hidden keyword blocks, doorway pages, repeated country pages or content that visitors cannot access through normal navigation.

## Primary clinic entity

Use the same clinic identity across the website:

- Dr Tammi Quek Developmental & Behavioural Paediatrics Clinic
- 10 Sinaran Drive, #10-12 Novena Medical Center, Singapore 307506
- +65 6397 6637
- Clinic WhatsApp +65 9627 4893

Do not publish unconfirmed hours, email addresses, qualifications, awards, ratings or testimonials.

## Page intent

- Homepage: developmental and behavioural paediatrics in Singapore
- Services: what the clinic may provide and its scope
- Concerns: plain-language overview without encouraging self-diagnosis
- Resources: preparation and process information
- International Families: overseas enquiry process for a Singapore consultation
- Schools & Professionals: authorised collaboration and manual coordination
- FAQ: concise answers that can be extracted accurately by answer systems
- Contact: human-reviewed appointment request

## Answer-engine structure

Each page begins with:

1. one descriptive H1;
2. a direct introductory answer;
3. clear sections using question or topic headings;
4. factual calls to action;
5. links to related pages.

FAQ structured data must match visible questions and answers exactly.

## Generative search

Generative systems are supported through clear entity facts, explicit service scope, authorship context, content-review controls and structured information. There are no special hidden prompts or unsupported claims.

## International search

Indonesia, the Philippines and Malaysia are mentioned naturally on the single International Families page. Do not create separate location pages unless the clinic later develops genuinely different, substantial information for those audiences.

## Medical content quality

- Avoid guaranteed outcomes.
- Avoid “best”, “leading”, “top” and similar superiority claims.
- Do not publish patient testimonials or ratings.
- Do not add Review or AggregateRating schema.
- Keep educational content separate from individual medical advice.
- Record the date and person responsible for clinical review before production launch.

## Launch checks

- Validate titles and descriptions.
- Confirm one H1 per page.
- Check canonical URLs under `/paediatrics-clinic/`.
- Validate JSON-LD.
- Submit `sitemap.xml` through Search Console.
- Confirm staging and local environments remain `noindex`.
- Confirm production is indexable only after clinic approval.
- Review search performance by page and country without sending enquiry data to analytics.
